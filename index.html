Web scrapers!

Many of them!

Dapodikdasmen, Sekolah Kita, and Verval Yayasan are school portals with one thing in common: no API. So each gets its own scraper, built around how the site actually behaves. Dapodikdasmen gives up school listings district by district with retry logic and batch CSV, Sekolah Kita profiles come down by NPSN code with checkpoint and resume, and Verval Yayasan gets crawled six levels deep with async Python. Together they feed one dataset, which turned into InternProject-SPP_Sekolah, the tuition prediction project, and then tanya-sekolah, the LLM assistant built on top of it.

PDDIKTI is educational but a different purpose: the national higher education database, universities rather than schools. Scraped across semesters with a multithreaded Selenium setup.

Separate from all of that: LinkedIn jobs, scraped with login persistence, random delays, and anti-detection.
